# Glossary of Grammatical Terms

- [[absolutive]]
- [[adjective]] — describes a noun or a pronoun
- [[example]]
- [[gerund]] — a form that is derived from a verb but functions as a noun. In English, the gerund ending is *-ing*
- [[noun]] — names a place, person, thing, or idea
- [[plural]]
- [[verb]] — a word that describes an action
